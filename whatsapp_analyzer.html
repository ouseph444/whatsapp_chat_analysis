How to export

  1. Open the chat in WhatsApp
  2. Menu → More → Export chat
  3. Choose Without media
  4. Save the file, then drop it here

What works

Group chats and one-to-one chats. iPhone and Android export layouts, 12- and 24-hour clocks, D/M/Y and M/D/Y dates, and any language — words are counted in every script, not just Latin.

Privacy

The file is read with the browser's local file API and analysed in memory. There is no server, no network request, no storage. Close the tab and it is gone.

Method & caveats

  • Participation is message count. Reactions are not included in WhatsApp exports, so someone who mostly reacts with ❤️ looks quieter here than they are in the chat.
  • Media are placeholders. An export without media stores lines like image omitted; the counts are exact but the content cannot be analysed.
  • Replies are inferred from sequence, not from WhatsApp's quote metadata, which exports drop. A "reply" is a message from a different person following someone else's, and response times are capped at 6 hours so overnight gaps don't distort the medians.
  • Conversations are runs of messages separated by more than 60 minutes of silence.
  • Timestamps are local to the phone that exported the chat, and daylight-saving shifts are not corrected. Ambiguous dates (both parts ≤ 12) fall back to D/M/Y — use the Dates control to override.
  • System notices (group created, renamed, people added or removed, pinned messages) and assistant replies from Meta AI are excluded from participation and reported separately.
  • Deleted messages survive as a placeholder: they count as a message but contribute no words.
